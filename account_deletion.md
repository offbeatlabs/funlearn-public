# Delete your Funlearn account and data

Offbeat Labs provides Funlearn. You can request deletion even if the app is no longer installed.

Effective date: 9 September 2026.

**In the app:** Open Settings > Privacy & data > Delete an existing account. Sign in to your existing account, then choose Delete account permanently.

**By email:** Send a request to [ofbtlabs@gmail.com](mailto:ofbtlabs@gmail.com?subject=Funlearn%20account%20and%20data%20deletion%20request) from the email address associated with your account. Include “Funlearn account and data deletion request” in the subject. If you want help with purchase-related data, include your Google Play order reference. Never send your password or payment-card details.

We may ask for information needed to verify that the account belongs to you. Deleting an authentication account removes its Firebase sign-in and profile. You can also request deletion of associated support records and RevenueCat purchase/account data. We retain those records while needed to provide or restore access and process support requests; after a verified deletion request, records are removed except where retention is required for accounting, fraud prevention or legal obligations. We will explain any applicable retention when handling the request. Account deletion does not automatically refund purchases or cancel Google Play subscriptions. Cancel a subscription separately in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Reading activity and any saved reader libraries from earlier versions are stored on your device. To remove them, use Settings > Privacy & data > Clear reading data. Remove any previously exported backup files and manage Android backups separately. Removing local reading data does not revoke an existing Play purchase or reset the current daily free allowance.

See the [Funlearn privacy policy](https://github.com/offbeatlabs/funlearn-public/blob/main/privacy_policy.md) for details about data use and service providers.
